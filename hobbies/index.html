Off the clock

Hobbies

When I'm not working with data at my job, I'm usually using it to keep my plants alive.

🌱 Automating plant care

My favorite side project is a small system that takes care of my plants for me. Sensors in the soil measure moisture, a microcontroller decides when a plant is thirsty, and a little pump waters it automatically. Every reading is logged, so I can chart how each plant is doing and tune the system over time.

It mixes everything I enjoy: electronics, a bit of code, and data analysis, plus the satisfaction of seeing something green grow.

  • IoT

How it works

  1. 📡

    Sense

    Sensors read soil moisture every few minutes.

  2. 💾

    Log

    Readings are saved, ready to analyze and chart.

  3. 🧠

    Decide

    If moisture drops below a threshold, it's time to water.

  4. 💧

    Water

    A small pump delivers just the right amount of water.
